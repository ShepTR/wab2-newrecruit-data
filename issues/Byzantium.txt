Byzantium: Beyond the Golden Gate (Warhammer Historical, 2005) - issues and open points
=====================================================================================
Built: 9 catalogues (Early Byzantines, Sassanid Persians, Germanic Kingdoms, Steppe Nomads, Bulgars & Slavs,
Early Caliphates, Thematic Byzantines, Rus, Late Byzantines). Scenario orders of battle (pp.96-111) are not built.
Everything is untested in New Recruit; the game system is at revision 14.

General
- Documented only (not enforced): allies and mercenaries drawn from another list (Steppe Nomads allies for the
  Byzantine lists, Rus Era of the Princes allies, Slav/Bulgar subjects of the Avars, Italo-Lombard Aghlabid Ghulams,
  Appendix I western enemies and allies, AoA Crusader list), Combined Formations (spearmen+archers, Kavallarioi+
  Hippo-toxotai, Kataphraktoi archers), "at least N units of X" minimums, Rivalry (Caliphates), Stratagem, Balkan
  Ruse palisades, the Themata/Tagmata army type (Thematic Byzantines), Prokousatores/Menaulatoi unit counts.
- Page numbers recorded on rules/units are printed page numbers and are approximate for some generic rules.
- OCR was used for the whole book; stat lines were checked against the page images for the Bulgar/Slav, Caliphate and
  Thematic lists. Spot-check anything that looks odd.

Early Byzantines / Sassanids / Germanic Kingdoms / Steppe Nomads
- Steppe Nomads: equipment options are tribe-specific in the book (Avar, Early/Later Khazar, Magyar, Pecheneg, Cuman).
  They are one option table here with per-composition availability; options marked "included" are free and must be
  taken by hand if the tribe has them as standard equipment (the base profile carries only a hand weapon and bow).
- Steppe Nomads: Light Infantry (Tribesmen) is not available to Avars, Early Khazars (no infantry list is printed),
  Magyars or Cumans. Subject Tribesmen, Alan Nobles and Alan Tribesmen are modelled as Allies and Mercenaries.
- Steppe Nomads: "Great Khagan" (+25, Ld 10) is allowed only for armies over 2,000 points - not enforced.
- Sassanids: the small leftover empty Army General entry that was created and removed while writing the data is harmless.
- Germanic Kingdoms: Vandal "Feigned Flight" cost changes use set-modifiers for the listed compositions only.

Bulgars & Slavs
- Slav infantry/cavalry caps inside Bulgar armies (33% / 25%) are documented only; the Slav characters are not
  available to Bulgar armies and the Bulgar characters are not available to Slav armies.
- Brigands: not available to Later Balkan Slavs. Brigand Leadership printed as 4 (as Warriors); check the page image.
- The Palisade Fence (18" at 4 pts per inch, Later Bulgars) is not built.
- "Two units of Slav Warriors may have halberds or double-handed weapons" is not limited to two units.

Early Caliphates
- Eight compositions in one catalogue: Arab Conquest, Umayyad, Early and Later Abbasid, Aghlabids, Tulunids &
  Ikhshidids, Early Fatimid and Hamdanids. The Fatimid and Tulunid/Ikhshidid "Infantry and Cavalry up to 66%" is a
  combined cap in the book; it is applied to Cavalry and Infantry separately here.
- Hamdanid Heavy Cavalry are shown in Cavalry; in Later Abbasid armies the book lists two units under Special Units
  "as well". Here they count in the Cavalry allocation (max two units in Later Abbasid).
- Ghulams are a secondary pool (Turkish, Armenian and Greek Ghulams, Daylamites) that counts in the Ghulams limit.
- Naffatun (one per City Militia skirmisher unit; two in Early Fatimid) is not enforced per unit.
- The Marzban needs a Khurasan Cavalry unit, so it is unavailable where no Khurasan unit can be taken.
- Umayyad armies list two units of Mountainmen although the unit entry says 0-1; the composition text was followed.
- "Superior Arab Cavalry (+2 pts)" for Regular Cavalry was read as applying to both Early and Later Abbasid armies.
- Camel Riders, Riding Camels and Riding Horses options are priced options only; no profile change is applied.

Thematic Byzantines
- The Themata Army Standard Bearer entry prints "heavy armour (+2 pts)"; every other character prints +4. Recorded
  as +4 pts - please check against the book.
- BS4 bow-armed units counting as BS3 (-1 pt) in Isaurian & Phrygian armies is documented only.
- Kataphraktoi archers are a second model line (0-1/3 of the unit) with no enforced limit.

Rus
- Rus Berserker is in Characters (0-3 per army here) with the "only in Varjazi units" restriction not enforced.
- The Warlord must contain a Malaia Druzhina or Varjazi unit (depending on the list) - not enforced.
- Chernye Klobuki per Tribal/Militia Light Cavalry ratio and Militia >= Rus Skirmishers ratio are not enforced.

Late Byzantines
- The Macedonian Successors may draw up to two Themata commanders from the Thematic Byzantines list - not built.
- Prokousatores for Macedonian Successors is on Irregular Kavallarioi (the "Light Pronoiarios" unit is not a separate
  entry); check how you want to represent Light Pronoiarios.
- Guard Kavallarioi / Emperor's Guards lance option removes Superior status and combined archers - not enforced.
