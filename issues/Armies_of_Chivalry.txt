ARMIES OF CHIVALRY - issues to circle back on (source: scan/OCR + visual read of page images; stats read from page images)
- Mounted/dismounted: built as separate unit entries; the 'mounted free, dismounted +N' plate armour costs are two labelled options each. Light armour and 'Heavy armour' options are now mutually exclusive (costs are absolute in the book; English HYW Spearmen heavy armour set to +3 over base).
- Armour saves/movement penalties follow the book's classes via the existing profiles, but equip_save uses the rulebook engine so the displayed Armour Save may differ for AoC (e.g. full plate 3+ on foot / 4+ mounted, +1 mounted, partial plate shield only on foot).
- Mounted-by-default units no longer show a Warhorse/Horse model profile (the Horse/Warhorse weapon entries were dropped as there is no gst profile); only 'Ride a warhorse' options carry the Warhorse profile.
- 'Every second unit' / 'one unit only' / 'up to half' rules are not enforced except where noted as once-per-army (Elite/Casa/Banderium/Gewalthut/Oriflamme/Familia Ducale; every-second-unit upgrades on Gendarmes, Skirmishers, Boieri Curteni are NOT enforced).
- 'At least one unit' (1+) minimums not enforced (Razesi, Knights, Mercenary Knights, Regular Pronoiarios when Elite/Guards chosen, Junior Druzhniks when Senior chosen, Latinikoi infantry needs Knights, Handgunner/Skirmisher minimums in Late Venetian/Milanese Condotta, Tartar Warriors per Tartar Noble).
- Per-model ratios not enforced: one Coustillier and 1+ Longbowman per Ordonnance Gendarme (French Ordonnance); Voulgiers/Pavisiers exclusivity (French HYW); Standard Bearer-only-with-hand-weapon restrictions; Hatman may only lead Western Men-at-Arms.
- HRE: Mercenaries category assignment and the 0-2/0-3 mercenary unit limits are approximations; Landsknecht/Verlorene Haufe options per-model costs approximated.
- Condotta: Italian state restrictions (Naples, Milan, Florence, Papal States, Venice, Venetian Greek) are documented in unit text only; Carrocio details and Casa/Familia Ducale 'cannot outnumber' limits not enforced; Early/Late period picker only.
- Moldavia/Wallachia: Razesi (Moldavia only) and Wagon Tabor (Moldavia only) enforced via picker; 'no Western Men-at-Arms or Szekels if Ottoman allies' is allies-only so documented.
- Russia: Western-army Senior/Junior Druzhniks built as separate '(Western)' entries; 'Western Knights' approximated from the Teutonic Crusader Knights entry (not copied exactly).
- Ottomans: Ghazi options per-model +1; Master Gunners (+20/cannon) and frontal plates (+10/cannon) documented only; Akincis availability by composition assumed (Turkoman in Ghazi, Akincis in Conquest); Janissary 'only with an Army General' and Kapikulu 'only with an Army General' not enforced; Solaks must be the smallest Janissary unit (not enforced). Balkan/Anatolian Tribesmen use the rulebook Mountain Tribesmen stats (no double-handed weapons).
- Byzantines: Permanent Mercenaries upgrade (+2 per model, one unit per 1,000 pts) built as a per-model option with no count limit; 'one Regular Psiloi unit may take crossbow' not enforced; Barbarian Warriors not built (Mountain Tribesmen only).
- Wars of the Roses: Welsh Spearmen armour entry is ambiguous in the book (light armour assumed); Mixed Order pairings documented only.
- Hussites: Polish allies documented only; war wagon cost 150 pts overrides the generic 120; tarasnices (palisade gun, +20) not built.
- Swiss: Command by Committee Captains built as flat +20 per unit (not exclusive of a Leader); Duke only with Allied Men-at-Arms not enforced; Gewalthut 'largest unit' not enforced.
- Wagon Tabor / War Wagon / Artillery: Master Gunner not built as a model; batteries not enforced; tabor wagon defender mixes documented only.
- Allies and mercenary contingents documented only (Free Companies, Swiss, Polish, Hungarian, Ottoman, Moldavian/Wallachian, Wars of the Roses, Lithuanian etc.).
- Scenarios (Shrewsbury etc.), campaign material and optional rules not built.
- Everything is untested in New Recruit.
