The Art of War (Warhammer Historical, 2006) - issues and open points
=====================================================================
Built: 14 catalogues (Early Chinese, Early Eastern Tribes, Zhou Dynasty, Early Horse Nomads, Warring States, Han Dynasty,
Han Red Eyebrows, Han Yellow Scarves, Han Three Kingdoms, Steppe Wusun/Xiongnu/Yuezhi/Xianbei/Wuhuan). Scenarios and the
Chengpu / Malin Pass history are not built. Everything is untested in New Recruit; the game system is at revision 17.
Stat lines were read from full-resolution page images, but the PDF is a scan with no text layer, so spot-check anything odd.

General
- Documented only (not enforced): Ruses (50 pts each, one per 1,000 pts, Military Strategist) and the Military Strategist
  rules; all Allies (other lists, Eastern Han, Han Chinese, Qiang, Wuhuan); "may only have one (or two) selected option per
  1,000 points" for Special Units; "at least 2 units of Horse Archers" in the steppe lists; Combined Formation 50% limits;
  "Archers must be added to Spearmen/Halberdiers" (Zhou); "must be the smallest unit" (Noble Guard, Eagleshooters);
  chariot mounts for characters (bought from the Chariotry list, not linked); Mob/Levy minimums.
- Lamellar armour is a new shared profile (5+ infantry save, cavalry 4+). The save calculation treats it as heavy armour for
  saves but as light armour for movement penalties.
- Horses are M8 in this book.
- "Steppe Nomad Cavalry", "Fast Pass" and the Caravan/Yurt Camp victory-point rules are carried as text, not mechanics.
  Fast Pass is a free option on the Wusun Horse Archers with no rule link; check the rules text.
- Repeating Crossbow: Strength 4 as printed (24" range, fires twice at -1 to hit).
- Caravan and Yurt Camp are built as machine/crew style units in each steppe catalogue (Yurt 3-5 at 5 pts, Defenders 4 pts,
  up to 20); the "four defenders per yurt" cap is not enforced per yurt.

Early Chinese / Early Eastern Tribes
- Frightening Masks (+20) is an option on the characters; its fear effect is documented only.
- Early Eastern Tribes: stone dolmen and spirit stone totems are modelled as units; the totem rules are not mechanised.

Zhou Dynasty
- Rightful Conduct and the Duke of Song apply to the Spring and Autumn period only; the Western Zhou composition still shows
  them (Duke of Song is made unavailable there; Rightful Conduct is only text on the characters).
- Duke of Song's cost (156) includes his chariot and archer; they are not modelled separately.
- Noble Guard: "light armour" is in the base equipment; the dagger axe replaces thrusting spear and shield.

Warring States
- State variants that change Special Unit availability or the Chariotry & Cavalry cap are built as compositions; the frenzy,
  drummer, stubborn, heavy armour, Noble Sword and Inspiring Red Banners variants are documented only.
- The Light Cavalry Lancers (0-1) are M7 here (M8 in the Han list) as printed.
- Stone Thrower crew cost is printed '??'; treated as 0 with a light armour option (+2 pts) for the crew.
- Tortoise Wagon is a separate unit; it is not attached to an infantry unit.
- Meng Tian: no restriction on the state variant is printed; he is available in all compositions.

Han Dynasty / Red Eyebrows / Yellow Scarves / Three Kingdoms
- Left Handed Fighting Style is an option on Han Elites (+2 pts); the effect is documented only.
- Red Eyebrows: the Mob minimum (one per 1,000 pts) is not enforced; fear of horses and Rousing the Mob are text only.
- Three Kingdoms: Sworn Brotherhood benefits (taking all three brothers together) are documented only.

Steppe lists
- Xiongnu Shanyu: the printed options list both 'light armour (+2 pts)' and 'lamellar armour instead of light armour (+2 pts)'; read as the lamellar upgrade only. Modun is M4 with a free 'Ride a horse' option (M8).
  read as the lamellar upgrade only. Modun's M is printed 4 (8 mounted).
- Wuhuan Han Chinese Auxiliaries (Special Unit) are included as a unit in the Wuhuan list.
