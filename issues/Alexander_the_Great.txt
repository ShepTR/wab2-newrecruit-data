ALEXANDER THE GREAT - issues to circle back on (source: scan/OCR + visual read; stats read from page images)
- Mounted-vs-foot weapon options (thrusting spear free on foot but +2 mounted; pike foot-only; xyston mounted-only) are entered as flat options and cannot be tied to mounting. Thrusting spear on Macedonian characters is entered as +2.
- Hypaspist and Pezhetairoi 'optional equipment' sets (pike/large-shield Hypaspists at M4; Pezhetairoi with javelins at 7 pts) and 'all units must choose the same' are not built as options.
- 'Every second unit' / 'up to half' rules not enforced (Mercenary skirmisher bows, Psiloi missiles, Thracian/Persian/Indian variants, Takabara/Mardian, Kyrtians, Nobles, Skirmish Cavalry).
- War machines: 'one per phalanx / infantry unit' not enforced; batteries of three not enforced.
- Philip II: cavalry limit drops to 25% when he is present - not enforced. His printed A2 is entered as 2 (A4 W4 per OCR: row read as 4 6 5 4 4 4 5 2 9).
- Epigonoi vs Pezhetairoi exclusivity (Imperial list) not enforced; the Indian Elephant (EX) 'needs another (EX) unit' not enforced.
- Hypaspist ratio: 1 per Pezhetairoi unit (Rise) / 1 per 2 (Imperial) enforced as a ratio.
- Thracians: Getic composition entered as cavalry at least 33% with no maximum; Getic 'bow +4 / +10 total' entered as a single +10 option on the King only (other Getic characters not covered). Mountain-per-two-Lowland ratios not enforced. Warlord 'Highland' needs Warlord as General (enforced by availability).
- Thracian Rolling Wagons: stats read as 3D6 M, A D6; entered as printed; launch rules in the rule text only.
- Illyrians: 'Slave Warriors not outnumbering warriors' enforced only as one slave unit per warrior unit; skirmishers vs warriors not enforced.
- End of Persia: Great King flee/panic rules documented in the rule text only. Persian Cavalry/Kinsmen sit under the 'Persians' category; Levies 50%; Allies & War Machines 15% (Lykians/Carians included there). Satrap/General restrictions (one per regiment) not enforced.
- Scythed chariot entered as one model (driver included in the notes only, driver model set to 0 count).
- Frontier Horsemen: no percentage for Characters printed; none entered. Cavalry at least 50%, Warriors up to 25%.
- Indian: character Movement 6 when mounted and mount costs (chariots, elephants) are not added automatically; King/Senapati/Padika mount choice not built. Elephants above four not enforced. Historical characters not built. Optional equipment mixes (half of Hereditary with shields etc.) not enforced.
- Mauryan Hereditary raised stats (WS3 I3, 10 pts) implemented as per-composition changes; Mauryan allies/foreign mercenaries documented only.
- Allies (Greek Mercenaries for Macedon/Persia, Thracians, Illyrians, Mountain Indians, Eastern Satrapies, Bithynians) documented only; Greek Mercenaries and Thracians are also stand-alone catalogues.
- Historical characters (Antipater, Antigonos, Darius III, Nabarzanes, Bessus, Porus etc.), campaigns, scenarios and optional rules (Prepared Ground, Out of Control, Elephant proximity) not built.
- Everything is untested in New Recruit.
