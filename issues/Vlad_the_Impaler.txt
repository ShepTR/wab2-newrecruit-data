Vlad the Impaler (Warhammer Historical, 2007) - issues and open points
======================================================================
Built: 9 catalogues (Hungary, Moldavia & Walachia, Ottomans, Serbia, Return of the Bulgars, Albania, Byzantine Empire,
Achaian Franks, Catalan Company). Scenarios (pp.99-130), sample rosters and the optional medieval rules (pp.133-134)
are not built. Everything is untested in New Recruit; the game system is at revision 15.

General
- The book is a scan; OCR was used for the text and the stat lines were read from the page images. Spot-check anything odd.
- Documented only (not enforced): allies and mercenaries drawn from other lists (every list has an Allies paragraph),
  "at least N units of X" minimums (e.g. 2+ Strelac, 1+ Boieri, 2+ Harketar, 1+ Lefegii per 1,000 pts), size-based
  Mehterhane limits, Vornic only in armies below 2,000 pts, per-period date restrictions on units.
- Per-1,000-point limits are modelled as "one per full 1,000 pts" (same convention as earlier books). Where the book says
  "0-1 per first 2,000 pts, then 0-1 per each 1,000" (Kapikulu Suvarileri, European Mercenary Knights) the first 2,000 are
  approximated as one per 1,000.
- Artillery crew: the book prints crew Movement as 8 in several tables (Sahaloz, Mangonel p.132); 4 was used.
- Mangonel profile and crew come from the special rules page (p.132): S7 T7 W3, crew WS2. The Kingdom of Hungary Katapulta,
  Romanian Catapulta and Ottoman Al Manjanik use the same profile.
- Bombard and mortar stat lines are printed shifted by one column in several tables. They were read as S/T/W
  (Light Bombard S6 T5 W3, Heavy Bombard S7 T7 W3, Ottoman Bacalusa S10 T7 W3, Havayi mortar S4 T5 W3).

Hungary
- The Allies and Mercenaries percentage is partly unreadable in the scan (25% used for all three lists).
- Count Jean de Nevers' Initiative is hard to read; 4 was used.
- The Pap's Leadership reads 6; Frenzy applies within 12 inches (modelled as the Frenzy (Religious Personality) rule text only).
- King Mátyás's Black Army minimums (25% allies, 1 war wagon per 1,000 pts, 2+ Hussite handgunner units) are documented only.
- Crusading Knights vs Sergeants (one Knights unit per Sergeants unit) is not enforced.
- Hunyadi and Mátyás are limited to the New Men / Crusader compositions as read from the text (Hunyadi: New Men and Crusader; Mátyás: New Men; Jean: Crusader).

Moldavia & Walachia
- The Tun (Bombard) stat line is printed as T3 W5 I3 (shifted); read as S3 T5 W3. It looks like a misprint.
- Vlad's bodyguard (Garda Domneasca with partial plate, 16/30 pts) is not priced separately; take the normal Garda entry
  and add armour options by hand.
- Great Host Walachia lists Pedestrasi, Arcasi and Plaiesi (no Razesi); Razesi are Moldavia only.

Ottomans
- Gazi State has no stated percentage for Kapikulu & Special Troops; only the Al Manjanik is available there.
- 0-4 Mehterhane is enforced as a flat maximum of four.
- Silahtars / Sipahi Oglan upgrade (needs a Padişah as General) is limited to one unit but not tied to the Padişah.
- Ditch & Stakes and Tethered Camels options have no per-unit limit beyond what the book prints.

Serbia
- Kingdom: "max one lance-armed unit per bow-and-spear unit"; Empire: "max one bow-and-spear unit per lance-armed unit" are not enforced.
- Special Troops in the Kingdom and Empire have no printed percentage; only the Mangonel is available.
- Despotates: Vlastella are lance-armed only.

Bulgars / Albania / Byzantine Empire / Franks / Catalans
- Bulgars: the Pronija Retainers <= Provincial Pronija ratio is enforced; the 1+ Horse Archers per 1,000 pts is not.
- Albania: Skanderbeg's era limits are not stated in the scan and were not applied.
- Byzantine Empire: Kavallarioi (dismounted) Movement printed as 8; used 4. Permanent Mercenaries is an option on each ally unit.
- Franks: Crossbowmen BS3/Ld7 upgrade is a priced option only (profile not changed); Baron "additional attack (+20)" is a priced option.
- Catalans: Almughavars! rules are text only (no rule effect).
