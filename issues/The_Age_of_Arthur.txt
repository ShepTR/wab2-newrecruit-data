The Age of Arthur (Warhammer Historical, 2005) - issues and open points
======================================================================
Built: 10 catalogues covering all army lists (19 compositions). Scenario orders of battle, campaigns and appendices are not built.
Everything is untested in New Recruit; the game system is at revision 16.

General
- Documented only (not enforced): allies and the reliability rule, the maximum of two Character Advantages for an Army General and one for
  other characters, each advantage only once per 1,000 points and once per character, General-only advantages (Born to the Purple, Loyalty,
  Man of the People, Chieftain Chariot) being restricted to an Army General, one Army Standard Bearer across several character types
  (it is limited per character type only), "each unit must be led by a Dux/Antrustio" for Pueri, light armour on at most half of the Franci units,
  Gedriht unit counts, the Saint Germanus / Dux-needs-a-Bishop rules, optional larger-shield rules for the Heptarchy Saxons, the Pictish thrusting spear option,
  King's Champion and Heroes in Chariots (Scots-Irish), Boats (units carried), Concealment placement.
- Horses are M7 in this book (not M8): the horse option sets M7 in the Arthur catalogues (done as a post-processing step in tools/arthur_limits.py).
  Ponies (M6) are the base profile of mounted Combrogi, Duguth, Geoguth, Pictish Nobles/Warriors, Fianna; the "Ride a horse (replaces ponies)" option raises them to M7.
- Foot units described as Light Troops carry the Light Troops rule; in the British Kingdoms composition Teulu and Combrogi on foot lose that status (documented in the unit text only).
- Page numbers are printed page numbers.

Romano-British Civitates
- Pedyt prints W3 (and T3) in the stat line (p.27, re-checked); this is almost certainly a misprint, so W1 is used.
- Magnate: Strength 3 and Toughness 3 as printed (p.26); Tribune S4 T3 W2.
- Brittany: "if the army includes a Dux/Comes it must also include a Bishop" is not enforced.
- Age of Vortigern: Saint Germanus (Bishop upgraded to Army General +70 with Man of the People and Loyalty) is an option on the Bishop; "no allies" is not enforced.

British and Welsh Kingdoms
- Teulu are listed 0-1; Combrogi (foot and mounted) and Pagenses have no limit. The Teulu foot/mounted pair is limited to 0-1 combined.
- Thrusting spears (Welsh Kingdoms in the West) are shown for foot Teulu (free) and foot Combrogi (+1) in every composition and are only available in the West composition.

Saxons
- Gedriht: the Sea Raiders entry says the usual limit of 0-1 Gedriht unit is removed, but the Gedriht entry itself does not print "0-1"; no unit limit is applied in any Saxon catalogue (the 33% Gedriht limit in the Sea Raiders list is not enforced).
- Saxon Buckler (+1 save, re-roll 1s, M5) is a rule on the models; the Movement characteristic is printed at 5.
- Cyning, Christian Priest and Army Standard Bearer are not available to the Sea Raiders. The Wiglera/Priest "either or" is enforced for the Early Anglo-Saxon and Anglian lists.

Franks
- Allied or alternative to the Early Merovingian Franks list in Beyond the Golden Gate (kept separate).
- Antrustio can take Angones et Franciscae (+30) only if not upgraded to Army Standard Bearer: not enforced.

Picts
- Chieftain BS6 (p.65) and the Arrchogad/Wolfhound rows (p.77) re-checked against the page scans: data matches print. The Pictish Packmaster row (5 3 3 3 4 1 4 1 6) has not been re-checked.
- Christian Priest is not available to the Early Southern Pictish Kingdoms or Picts of the North; Attecotti list is silent, so both priests are allowed.
- Attecotti: all Nobles and Warriors are Fierce (even mounted) - documented only. Nobles may not have horses or light armour in the North and Attecotti (enforced).
- Hunting crossbow is an option on Pictish Hunters (one unit, +1 pt) with no weapon profile; use the rule text in the catalogue note.

Scots-Irish
- Light chariot with driver (+10, Curadh/Toiseach) and Chieftain Chariot (+50) change M to 8 and A to 4 (3 for Toiseach); the chariot crew and rules are not modelled.
- Ri Tuath is unavailable to Raiders and Settlers; the Druid is unavailable to Dalriada; Dalriada may not field Ridir (mounted Ceithern).
- Kingdoms of Ireland: the 40% Characters limit with a Heroes in Chariots squadron is not enforced (33% is applied).
