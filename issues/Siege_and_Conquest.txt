Siege & Conquest (Warhammer Historical, 2007) - issues and open points
======================================================================
Built: nothing. The book is a rules supplement (siege equipment, settlements, assaults, siege characters, border skirmish rules, scenarios,
campaign rules) and has no army lists: pp.7-19 are a short history by era, pp.22-32 siege equipment and siege troops, p.63 siege characters.
Siege troops and characters are optional additions to existing lists and are not built as separate catalogues.
Open: siege engines, Siege Troops and siege characters could be added later as a shared "Siege Supplement" catalogue if wanted.
