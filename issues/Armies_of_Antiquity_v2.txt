Armies of Antiquity v2024 (unofficial, Martin Gibbins; "AoA2") - issues and open points
====================================================================================
Built: 125 catalogues (one per army list, file names *_AoA2.cat), 1505 unit entries. Game system revision 18.
Source: "Armies of Antiquity v 2024.pdf" (updated 01 January 2024, includes the v2.0 army lists, FAQs and list errata). It replaces the earlier .docx version of the lists,
so the first generated set was regenerated from it (the errata/clarifications are already merged into the 2024 lists, so they were not applied separately).
Everything is untested in New Recruit.

IMPORTANT - how these lists were made
- Unlike the official books, these lists were NOT typed in by hand. tools/aoa24_extract.py reads the PDF text layout (tables + paragraphs) and tools/aoa2_gen.py turns it into tools/aoa2_data.py.
  tools/aoa2_extract.py is the older reader for the .docx version. The generated data is meant to be corrected by hand afterwards (re-running aoa2_gen.py overwrites it).
  Stat lines and points come straight from the source tables; weapons, options and special rules are parsed from the description text by heuristics and need a read-through, army by army.
- Known weak spots of the parser (check these on any list you use):
  * Option labels come from the text before each "(+N)": a few are clumsy or mislabelled (e.g. stat changes such as "Ld8", "WS3", "+1A" are priced options that do not change the profile;
    "Chariot as from the list below" and similar are priced notes only). A negative price is a real saving printed in the book (e.g. -2).
  * "Instead of" choices (X or Y) are listed as free options named "X (instead of Y)"; the base weapon is the first one listed and nothing removes it when the option is chosen.
  * Special rules are taken from the base description sentences; rules that apply only in some compositions, or only "if dismounted", are mostly left off or may be attached wrongly.
  * "Light Infantry/Cavalry/Camelry" is modelled with the Light Troops rule; "Skirmishers" uses the Art of War skirmisher rule; "Unreliable" allies carry the Allies and Mercenaries (Unreliable) rule.
  * Characters' mounts: "Ride a horse" / "Ride a warhorse" options with the printed cost; barding is a priced option only.
  * Machines: a machine model plus a crew model; crew counts are parsed from words ("two crewmen") and default to 2 when no number was found.
  * Unit sizes: minimum 5 models for infantry/cavalry (1 for characters, chariots, elephants, machines), no maximum. Leader/Standard/Musician options (+5 each) are on every troop unit except
    skirmishers and units whose text says they may not have a standard or musician; lists that state "no army or unit standards" (e.g. Lydian) have no Standard bearer option.
- Army size note from the source: 2,800 points of these lists is roughly 2,000 points of a period supplement. A Senior Officer may become the Army General for +50 (not built).
- Allies lists ("may take allies from the X list"), the Allies and Mercenaries guidelines in the errata (host list sets the points; compulsory units first; no war machines from allies), Wagon Tabors, Camps and
  per-army special deployment rules are documented only.

The last 23 lists in the file (Sumerian & Akkadian through Kushite Egyptian) are the WAB 2.0 Chariot Wars lists. They are built with the same script but their descriptions are laid out differently
(several units share one paragraph), so they are the least reliable of the set. They duplicate the official Chariot Wars catalogues (hand-built, more reliable); prefer those.

Lists most likely to need manual rework (odd source structure)
- Steppe Nomads (tribe-by-tribe equipment tables)
- Mongol Conquest and Khanates (several sub-armies share one layout; compositions/ally text not split)
- Ordensstaat, Swiss (custom groups; Vorhut/Nachhut upgrades not built)
- Crusaders, Russian, Hussite, Medieval French (custom group names)
- Hindu India ("Specials": one elephant per 600 points, at least one required - documented only)
