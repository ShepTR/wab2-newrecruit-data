Armies of Antiquity v2 (unofficial, Martin Gibbins) - issues and open points
============================================================================
Built: 101 catalogues (one per army list, file names *_AoA2.cat), 1276 unit entries. Game system revision 18.
Source: "02_Armies of Antiquity v 2.0.docx" (and the matching PDF). Everything is untested in New Recruit.

IMPORTANT - how these lists were made
- Unlike the official books, these 101 lists were NOT typed in by hand. tools/aoa2_extract.py reads the .docx (tables + italic rule markers) and tools/aoa2_gen.py turns it into tools/aoa2_data.py.
  The generated data is meant to be corrected by hand afterwards (re-running aoa2_gen.py overwrites it). Stat lines and points come straight from the source tables and are reliable;
  weapons, options and special rules are parsed from the description text by heuristics and need a read-through, army by army.
- Known weak spots of the parser (check these on any list you use):
  * Option labels come from the text before each "(+N)": a few are clumsy or mislabelled (e.g. stat changes such as "Ld8", "WS3", "+1A" are priced options that do not change the profile;
    "Chariot as from the list below", "Instead be deployed as Skirmishers" and similar are priced notes only). A negative price is a real saving printed in the book (e.g. -2).
  * "Instead of" choices (X or Y) are listed as free options named "X (instead of Y)"; the base weapon is the first one listed and nothing removes it when the option is chosen.
  * Special rules are taken from the base description sentences; rules that apply only in some compositions, or only "if dismounted", are mostly left off or may be attached wrongly.
  * "Light Infantry/Cavalry/Camelry" is modelled with the Light Troops rule; "Skirmishers" uses the Art of War skirmisher rule; "Unreliable" allies carry the Allies and Mercenaries (Unreliable) rule.
  * Characters' mount: "Ride a horse" / "Ride a warhorse" options with the printed cost; barding is a priced option only. M in the table is used as printed (some lists print the mounted value).
  * Machines: a machine model plus a crew model; extra crew options and crew counts are parsed from words ("two crewmen") and default to 2 when no number was found.
  * Unit sizes: the minimum is 5 models for infantry/cavalry units (1 for characters, chariots, elephants, machines); no upper limit. Leader/Standard/Musician options (+5 each) are on every troop unit except
    skirmishers and units whose text says they may not have a standard or musician; lists that state "no army or unit standards" (e.g. Lydian) have no Standard bearer option.
- Army size note from the source: 2,800 points of these lists is roughly 2,000 points of a period supplement. A Senior Officer may become the Army General for +50 (not built).
- Allies lists ("may take allies from the X list"), Wagon Tabors, Camps and per-army special deployment rules are documented only.

Lists most likely to need manual rework (odd source structure)
- Steppe Nomads (tribe-by-tribe equipment tables)
- Mongol Conquest and Khanates (four sub-armies share one layout; compositions/ally text not split)
- Ordensstaat (custom groups)
- Crusaders, Russian, Hussite, Swiss, Medieval French (custom group names)
