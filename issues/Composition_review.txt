Army composition review - all books - potential issues and questions
=======================================================================
Scope: every catalogue that has more than one army composition (87 of 246 catalogues). For each composition the new tool
tools/audit_compositions.py works out which units can actually be added once that composition is picked (not hidden, not capped
at 0) and compares that with the unit names in the composition's own text (the "ARMY COMPOSITIONS" note). Run it with
  python3 tools/audit_compositions.py --review [catalogue.cat ...]
Its output is a pointer, not a verdict: it flags things like "no Cavalry" next to a unit that is still available, and it also
flags false positives (for example a unit mentioned in a "may take X only if..." sentence). Every flag was read against the text.

B. QUESTIONS (need your decision or a source I do not have)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Balearic Slingers (Hannibal, Republican Roman): confirm "unavailable in every composition" stands unless you find an FAQ
   that adds them (pp.51-52 name them nowhere).
2. Italian Extraordinarii (Hannibal, Republican Roman): not named in any composition either. Same treatment as the Balearic
   Slingers, or are they allowed in some list?
3. AoA2 Italian City States, Later Italians: units are labelled F/M/P/N/V (Florence, Milan, Papal States, Naples, Venice) and "you
   may only take units allowed to your city state". There is only one Later composition, so every labelled unit is visible.
   Do you want five city-state compositions (more pickers), or leave it as text?
4. AoA2 Japanese: besides Early/Middle/Late there is a fourth composition called "Japanese" (the general rules entry). It hides
   nothing, so someone could pick it and get every unit. Should it be removed as a picker?
5. AoA2 Steppe Nomads: the "Infantry: None" caps (Cuman, Magyar) are left alone because the same text lets those armies take a
   few Subject Tribesmen units (Magyar up to two). Five of the nine compositions (Alan, Avar, Early Khazar, Huns, Late Khazar) have
   no composition text in the catalogue at all. Can you point me to the AoA2 text for them?
6. AoA2 compositions with no composition text in the catalogue (nothing to check against, so nothing is gated):
   Dark Age Britain and Ireland (5), Early Byzantine (Early/Later Army), Early Hoplite Greek (Athens/Sparta/Thebes), Early Imperial
   Roman (2), Gothic and Vandal Kingdoms (4), Israelite (2), Later Hoplite Greek (6), Later Irish (2), Mongol Conquest and
   Khanates (Jalayirids), Persians and Medes (2), Shang Chinese (2), Slav or Bulgar (4), Thracian (3). Is there a source, or do these
   lists simply have no per-period unit restrictions?
7. Byzantium: Bulgars and Slavs (all four compositions), Early Caliphates (Tulunids; Ikhshidids), Thematic Byzantines
   (Isaurian; Phrygian) have no composition text in the data, so only the percentage caps are in place. Was the text missing in
   the book, or did I miss it?
8. Shieldwall English: Kent and Home Counties (one composition) has no composition text.
9. Vlad the Impaler, Kingdom of Hungary (Arpad and Angevin composition) and Armies of Chivalry Moldavia and Walachia (two
   compositions) have no composition text.
10. Armies of Chivalry: Liberi in the Capetian and Breton lists - which compositions may take them?
11. AoA2 Arab Conquest, Umayyad Caliphate: "may not be led by a Companion". Companions are left available as ordinary
    characters. Correct, or hidden entirely?
12. Warring States, Chu: "Eastern Shaman only if no Military Strategist is taken" is documented in text, not enforced. Yan is
    enforced (strategist hidden). Do you want the Chu either/or enforced?
13. Leader-type restrictions ("no chieftain: led by a mormaer upgraded to army general", Scots-Irish "curadh", El Cid Al-Andalus
    Caliphate "not a Christian captain") are text only. Hide the character for those compositions?

C. NOT ENFORCED (rules the program cannot express, or ones not modelled; spot-check individual units, a few of these may
   already carry a ratio rule from the book's own limits script)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Partial limits: "only one unit of crossbowmen" (Al-Andalus Taifa, infantry crossbowmen), "no more than 2 units of Hindu cavalry"
  (Rajputs), "no more than one unit of Elite Cavalry" (Vijayanagar), "a maximum of one unit each of elite cavalry and horse
  archers" (Lodi), "only one unit of Hindu cavalry" (Tughluq), "at least half the cavalry must be Jinetes" (Al-Andalus),
  Ottoman ratios (Imam per Tribal Infantry, Tribal Infantry/Feudal Horse per 1,200 pts, stone thrower per 1,400 pts), "Cumans
  may not outnumber Lithuanians" (Later Polish), "at least one unit of Varjazi" (Pagan Rus).
- Option bans: "may not take heavy armour" (Early Christian Spanish), "no unit may take heavy armour" (Pagan Rus), "may not have
  barding" (Rajput characters and elite cavalry).
- Conditional rules: Carroccio only if no army standard bearer (Holy Roman Empire, Early City/Clerical); no Tartar nobles or
  warriors if western Russian states are taken; Sipahis and volunteers limits in the Ottoman Chivalry lists; "no armigeri" in
  the Italian condotta allies of the Hungarian-Crusader army (Vlad); thegns, gebur, hirdmen/bondir and similar unit-count
  rules in Shieldwall; Lusitanian/Caetrati ratios in the Spanish Hannibal list (partly enforced as a ratio).
- Allies "from the X list" lines are text only; allies cannot be linked across catalogues.
- Percentage caps per composition are applied as army-composition limits; they do not hide units, so a unit in a category
  capped at 0% stays visible (only the bans listed in section A hide units).
