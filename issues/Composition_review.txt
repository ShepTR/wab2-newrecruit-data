Army composition review - all books - potential issues and questions
=======================================================================
Scope: every catalogue that has more than one army composition (87 of 246 catalogues). For each composition the new tool
tools/audit_compositions.py works out which units can actually be added once that composition is picked (not hidden, not capped
at 0) and compares that with the unit names in the composition's own text (the "ARMY COMPOSITIONS" note). Run it with
  python3 tools/audit_compositions.py --review [catalogue.cat ...]
Its output is a pointer, not a verdict: it flags things like "no Cavalry" next to a unit that is still available, and it also
flags false positives (for example a unit mentioned in a "may take X only if..." sentence). Every flag was read against the text.

C. ENFORCED BY tools/rules_limits.py (since the Vijayanagar question) AND WHAT IS STILL NOT ENFORCED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enforced as validation (the roster shows an error when the limit is broken; table in tools/rules_limits.json, 231 rules in 99 catalogues):
- Unit counts per composition: "0-1 / no more than N units of X" (Hindu cavalry Orissa 1 / Rajput 2, Elite Cavalry Vijayanagar 1, Lodi
  elite cavalry and horse archers 1 each, Tughluq Hindu cavalry 1, Bahmani handgunners 2, Taifa and Middle Christian Spanish
  crossbowmen 1, Moldavian Nobles 1, Later Ottoman Tribal Infantry 1).
- Minimums: elephants (Orissa 2, Standard and Vijayanagar 1, Lodi and Tughluq 1), Albanian 2 Archers, Bulgarian 2 Horse Archers,
  Pagan Rus Varjasi (both Rus books), Ottoman horse archers / regular cavalry / feudal horse.
- Per-points limits: elephants (Orissa 1 per 450 + the general's mount; Standard, Vijayanagar 1 per 600; Muslim Indian 1 per 700),
  rocket launchers 1 per 1,400, Ottoman Tribal Infantry / Feudal Horse 1 per 1,200, stone thrower 1 per 1,400, Later Ottoman artillery
  1 per 900 (shared), Polish cannon 1 per 2,400, Later Hungarian wagons and cannon 1 per 1,000, Later Italian guns 1 per 1,000 (shared),
  Mongol Conquest artillery 1 per 1,400 (shared). Limits use whole units of points (floor), as in the rest of the data.
- Ratio: Ottoman Imam needs a unit of Tribal Infantry.
- Option limits: about 190 "every second / every third unit may ...", "one / two units may ..." sentences, e.g. bows, slings, light armour,
  Stubborn, Ld8 upgrades, Royal Guard, Brabancons, Military Order Knights. Options that share one allowance (sling OR bow) are enforced as
  one allowance for pairs; "every second" is ceil(units/2), so the first unit may always have the option.
Still NOT enforced (the program cannot express them, or the text is ambiguous):
- Allowances shared by three or more options (each option is limited on its own; the engine prints a note when it meets one).
- "Up to half / a third / a quarter of the figures" rules are now enforced as separate model lines (tools/loadout_split.py, 59 units; section E).
  Still plain unit-wide options: Mercenary Infantry (Later Byzantine, "up to two units instead"), Tang Skirmishers, Daylamite archer
  option, Landsknecht pike units (unit-level rule), Israelite Zebulunites ("half the unit"), Alexandrian Experimental Phalanx ("at least
  half archers"), Early Byzantine Bucellarii (AoA2), Archers joining spearmen units (Trojan, Minoan), Dismounted Milites' separate +1 bow.
- "May not outnumber" rules (Cumans vs Lithuanians, Normans vs Bretons, Tribal Infantry vs Religious Volunteers, Mongol Heavy vs Light
  cavalry models).
- Ratios that differ between compositions of one book (Serbia lance vs bow-and-spear units, Spanish Lusitanians vs Caetrati partly).
- Raised caps: Warring States Zhao 0-2 Light Cavalry Archers and Qin 0-2 Light Cavalry Lancers (the 0-1 cap stays).
- Unit-level "every second unit" rules (Greek AoA1 Theban / Thessalian cavalry, Alexander Successors Elite Light Cavalry, Comnenian guards).
- Persians and Medes (Cyrus) thrusting-spear sentence (ambiguous), tarasnices, El Cid mercenary sentences, Early Imperial Roman options,
  Assyrian Royal Guard (no matching option), Thematic Byzantines skirmisher bows (no option), handguns for Spanish Military Orders.
- Partial limits: "at least half the cavalry must be Jinetes" (Al-Andalus), Rajput "no barding", Early Christian Spanish "no heavy armour",
  Pagan Rus "no heavy armour".
- Conditional rules: Carroccio only if no army standard bearer (Holy Roman Empire); no Tartar nobles or warriors if western Russian
  states are taken; Sipahis and volunteers limits in the Ottoman Chivalry lists; "no armigeri" in the Italian condotta allies (Vlad);
  thegns, gebur, hirdmen/bondir and similar unit-count rules in Shieldwall.
- Allies "from the X list" lines are text only; allies cannot be linked across catalogues.
- Percentage caps per composition are applied as army-composition limits; they do not hide units, so a unit in a category
  capped at 0% stays visible (only the bans listed in section A hide units).
Rounding note: "every second unit may ..." allows the first unit to have it (ceil). If you read the books as "needs two units first" it is a
one-word change in tools/rules_limits.py.

E. FRACTIONAL LOADOUTS (version 0.1.1) - assumptions to check
-------------------------------------------------------------
Units are split into a base model line and one line per loadout (tools/loadout_split.json). Choices I made where the books are silent:
- Price: the difference printed in the book. Where no price is printed (Skutatoi archers, Hippo-toxotai, Toxotai, Greek Ghulam and
  Emperor's Guard archers, Western Franks' Dismounted Milites bow) the loadout costs the same as the base figure.
- Loadout lines drop the replaced weapons. Where the book says "spear and shield" for a bow, the shield (and large shield) is dropped and
  the armour save is rebuilt (none, or 6+ with light armour). Cavalry that only swap the spear keep their save.
- "May take no further equipment" is honoured by not charging the unit's other options on the loadout line; the program cannot stop
  the player ticking an option that applies to the whole unit, so the unit-wide armour/shield options still show.
- Hereditary infantry and Guildsmen (India): the bowmen are the base figures; "up to half may replace longbow with throwing spear,
  javelins and shield (+1)" is the loadout, and the large shield upgrade is charged only on those figures.
- Late Imperial Legionaries and Auxilia: composite bow and buckler (-3) for up to a third, locked.
- Western Franks (Shieldwall) mounted Milites are not covered ("including mounted troops").
