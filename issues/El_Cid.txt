EL CID - issues to circle back on (source: scan only, every stat row read visually)
- p.49 Almohads: Common Troops printed 'Up to 50%' (confirmed by Marc); entered as up to 50%.
- p.38 Later Taifas: characters may wear heavy armour for +3 pts, the same as the printed light armour price. Heavy armour entered as free upgrade over light armour (replaces light armour, +0).
- p.12 Bishop: light armour +3 / heavy armour +4; the two are exclusive (heavy is hidden once light is ticked, and vice versa).
- p.50 Emir: one entry (160 pts, 0-1) with a free 'Venerable Emir' option that changes the profile (WS4 BS3 S3 T3 I5 A2 Ld10).
- p.11-13 Characters: throwing spear and thrusting spear are exclusive; javelins stay independent.
- Army General options ('if no other Army General'): exclusivity with the inherent Generals (Rey, Caliph, Emir) and each other is enforced with option modifiers; untested in New Recruit.
- Aliados y Mercenarios: one unit led by the Army General is exempt from the unreliability rule; documented only.
- Andalusian Allies: documented only (no pick list), per Marc's rule for allies.
- Mixed weapon/'half of the units' limits not enforced: half of cavalry models Andalusian Jinetes; up to half of Andalusian Jinete units with bows; up to half of Berber Spearmen units Stubborn (Almohads).
- Horse and warhorse options are exclusive on every El Cid unit that has both.
- Campaign rules and scenarios not built.
